Because every story deserves to be written effortlessly.

  • Observer-Participant Model – Passive observation with shared activities

This pen is designed to help elderly individuals with arthritis or tremors write more comfortably and steadily.
  • Ergonomic grip reduces strain and ensures a secure hold,
  • Built-in gyroscopic system counteracts hand vibrations by generating stabilizing micro-vibrations.
The maze board complements the pen by helping users improve muscle control and coordination.
  • Fun maze and puzzles with unobtrusive feedback, encourages users to improve steady hand movements, turning rehabilitation and into an engaging activity.
Pen Enhancements
  • Different nibs for the pen, such as brushes and markers. Besides drawing and writing, users will be able to make art also.
  • Universal usage so that the pen can be used in any paper surface and not restricted to the surface
Future Prospects:
  • Additional Grid Feature: Alongside the maze, a standard writing grid will be added. If the user writes outside the lines, the surface will vibrate to provide real-time guidance.
  • Integrated Printer: A built-in printer will allow users to print and document their work from the LCD screen, creating a seamless blend of digital and physical outputs.

Testing and Feedback

"Embedding varying levels of difficulty in the maze could cater to users with different dexterity levels."

"Prolonged vibration-based stabilization may lead to muscle fatigue, limiting the product’s benefits over time."

"The ergonomic grip is a strong selling point, but a sleeker, lightweight design could further reduce hand fatigue and improve portability."
