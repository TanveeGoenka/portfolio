Designing

Rebranding

Bridging Generations. Empowering FuturesWelcome to the new identity of Indian Bank, where legacy meets innovation and trust meets transformation.This rebranding marks a pivotal step in the journey to become a future-ready digital bank, rooted in India’s rich heritage while embracing the needs of today’s diverse, dynamic generations. The refreshed identity is more than a visual update. It is a commitment:
  • To serve every generation with clarity
  • To simplify banking through technology
  • To stay human in a digital world.
This is the new Indian Bank. For your today, and their tomorrow.

Original IdentityThe original Indian Bank logo has long served as a symbol of the bank’s institutional identity. The use of traditional typography and golden tones emphasized credibility, heritage, and the bank’s national reach.However, over time, another logo variation emerged integrating elements from the merged Allahabad Bank, and differing treatments across branches.This inconsistency in branding weakens recall and dilutes the visual cohesion. A single, refreshed identity is essential to build stronger recognition, trust, and alignment across generations.

The central point is symbolic of focused and unified service

Depicts the flow of wealth between the customer, the nation, and the bank

Signifies expansion in all directions, aligning with the current tagline

Forming the shape of a banyan tree, it represents stability, unity, and growth

Redesigned IdentityThe new experience needed a new skin. The rebrand was built on the core concept of "Bridging Generations"
  • An Evolved Logo, retaining the core symbolism, and modernized, making it clean, confident, and digital-first.
  • A Generational Palette: The new colors tell a story. A "Primary Blue" for Gen X's trust, an "Accent Yellow" for Gen Z's optimism, and an "Energetic Orange" for Gen Alpha's creativity.
  • Human-Centered Imagery: I created guidelines to move away from static, posed photos to warm, authentic, "lived-in" moments that reflect diverse Indian families.
  • Clarity in Typography: We adopted Inter (for Latin) and Anek Devanagari (for Devanagari) two highly legible, modern, and accessible typefaces.
